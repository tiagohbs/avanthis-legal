Privacy policy

Avanthis Labs · CADEO · Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy explains how the CADEO app handles data. It applies to the Android app published by Avanthis Labs.

Summary

  • Avanthis Labs does not receive your projects, prompts, responses or credentials, except what you send in a report.
  • The app has no ads, analytics or crash reporting.
  • When you use an agent, your data goes to the AI provider you choose, with your account.

Data that stays on the device

The app stores projects, terminals, notes, settings and agent logins on your device. This data does not go to Avanthis Labs servers. Android automatic backup is turned off for the app.

If you sign in with Google, the account email and name stay only on the device, encrypted, to show which account you used. The date you accepted the terms also stays on the device.

Data is not separated by account: anyone who uses the app on this device sees the same projects, terminals and agent logins, with or without a Google account.

Data that leaves the device

When you use the app, these transfers happen through your actions:

  • AI agents: prompts, responses, attached images and project files the agent reads go to the chosen provider, such as Anthropic, OpenAI or Google.
  • Agent logins: your email and account details go to the account provider, in the agent's own flow.
  • App Google account: you choose the account in Google's own screen, under Google's policy. The app receives the email and name and does not send them to anyone.
  • Reports: when you report an agent response, the app opens your email addressed to Avanthis Labs. It carries the reason, the details, the terminal name, the agent, the provider, the date and, if you allow it, the response, with tokens and keys removed. As with any email, we also receive your address. We use it to assess the case and keep it for up to 12 months. When appropriate, we notify the provider with only the response and the reason, without your email.
  • Installations: requests for packages and skills go to services such as npm and GitHub.
  • Terminal and web preview: go to the addresses you use.

AI providers

Each provider has its own terms of use and privacy policy, which apply to your account. Check those of the provider you use. Avanthis Labs does not control these services or what they do with the data they receive.

These providers may process data outside your country.

On the free plans of some providers, such as Gemini, the provider may use prompts and responses to improve its models. Paid plans usually do not. Read the rules of the plan you use before sending confidential data.

Your choice

The app asks for your consent before the first use of an agent. Without it, the app works as a terminal and agents do not open. You can change this in Settings › Security. Turning it off does not erase what the provider already received.

Signing in with Google is optional. To sign out, use Settings › Security › Google account: the stored email and name are deleted, and everything else stays on the device. Removing the app's access at myaccount.google.com/connections ends the link on Google's side, but only signing out deletes what is on the device.

Other people's data

Your projects may contain personal data of third parties. You are responsible for what you send to agents.

Security

Communication between the app's internal parts stays on the device, protected by a token kept only in memory. Critical actions ask for fingerprint or PIN when the device has a screen lock. Agents communicate with providers over encrypted connections. What you type in the terminal may use any connection, including unencrypted ones.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on the device until you delete it. Use "Erase all data" in Settings › Data, which also removes the Google account, or uninstall the app. The record of your acceptance of the terms stays until you uninstall the app. For data held by a provider, use its channels.

Your rights

Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law 13.709/2018) grants rights such as confirmation, access, correction, deletion and withdrawal of consent. Google sign-in is based on your consent, which you withdraw by signing out. Apart from reports you send, Avanthis Labs does not receive your data, so most of these requests are handled in the app or with the provider. For questions, use the contact below.

Children

The app is a development tool and is not intended for children.

Changes

If the app starts handling new data, such as licensing or an Avanthis Labs server, this policy will be updated before the change.
